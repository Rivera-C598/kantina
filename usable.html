Help

Common questions about pre-ordering.

How do I order?

Add items from the Menu, open your Cart, choose a pickup time, then select Place order.

Can I change an order after placing it?

You can cancel an order while it says "Preparing," then place a new one.

Are there extra fees?

A ₱10 packaging fee is added once per order. It's always shown in the cart before you order.

What if an item is sold out?

Sold-out items are labeled on the menu and can't be added. Check again tomorrow.
